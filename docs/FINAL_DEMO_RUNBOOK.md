## 2. Step-by-Step 8-Stage Guided Demonstration Flow

### Step 1: Executive Overview & System Posture
- **Action:** Open the **`HOME`** screen.
- **Narrative:** *"REACT-X begins with a calm executive posture answering the 4 essential triage questions: What is happening? Where? How serious? What should we open next?"*
- **Highlight:** Show the national facility summary and system status banner.

### Step 2: Live Hotspot Detection & Spatial Attribution
- **Action:** Click **`MONITOR` $\to$ `Live Command Map`**.
- **Narrative:** *"Here we observe thermal anomalies clustered using H3 L9 spatial indexing, with automatic geometric attribution to plant boundary polygons and Copernicus 10m Land Cover."*

### Step 3: AI Source Discrimination (Flares vs Fires)
- **Action:** Click **`INTELLIGENCE` $\to$ `AI Classification`**.
- **Narrative:** *"REACT-X distinguishes routine operational flares and high-temperature process heat from uncontrolled runaway fires, filtering out solar glint reflections."*

### Step 4: Early-Warning Hazard Trajectory & Prediction
- **Action:** Click **`INTELLIGENCE` $\to$ `Hazard Trajectory`**.
- **Narrative:** *"Rather than waiting for catastrophic ignition, CUSUM change-point trend algorithms detect persistent thermal rate-of-rise ($dT/dt$) up to $60\,\text{minutes}$ in advance."*

### Step 5: Multimodal Evidential Fusion (Dempster-Shafer)
- **Action:** Click **`INTELLIGENCE` $\to$ `Evidence & Fusion`**.
- **Narrative:** *"Dempster-Shafer orthogonal combination synthesizes satellite infrared, plant telemetry, thermal cameras, and optical CCTV into a single explainable certainty score."*

### Step 6: Consequence Modeling & Atmospheric Dispersion
- **Action:** Click **`RESPONSE` $\to$ `Incident Assessment`**.
- **Narrative:** *"Real-time ALOHA heavy-gas dispersion equations project toxic and flammable plume boundaries driven by live local wind vectors."*

### Step 7: Safe Evacuation Routing & Tactical Resource Dispatch
- **Action:** Click **`RESPONSE` $\to$ `Evacuation Routing`** and **`Resource Dispatch`**.
- **Narrative:** *"Wind-aware Dijkstra routing calculates safe egress corridors for plant personnel while resource optimization allocates foam and water supplies."*

### Step 8: Pre-Plan Compliance PDF & Human Authorization
- **Action:** Click **`RESPONSE` $\to$ `Emergency Pre-Plan`** and click **`Export PDF`**.
- **Narrative:** *"The incident commander reviews all evidence and formally signs off. All recommendations are strictly advisory—human authority remains paramount."*
