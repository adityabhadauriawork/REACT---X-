# REACT-X — End-to-End Demonstration & Evaluator Guide

## Overview

This guide details how to demonstrate **REACT-X** (*Predictive Industrial Safety & Emergency Decision Platform*) to technical judges, industrial plant managers, and safety evaluators.

The platform demonstrates the complete safety lifecycle:
$$\textbf{PREDICT} \longrightarrow \textbf{PREVENT} \longrightarrow \textbf{PROTECT} \longrightarrow \textbf{PREPARE} \longrightarrow \textbf{RESPOND} \longrightarrow \textbf{LEARN}$$

---

## 1. Quick Launch (Running Locally)

### Backend (FastAPI + Python 3.12)
```bash
cd backend
uvicorn app.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000 --reload
```
- Interactive Swagger API Docs: `http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs`
- System Health Check: `http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/health`

### Frontend (React + Vite + Leaflet)
```bash
cd frontend
npm run dev
```
- Access Command Center: `http://localhost:5173`

## 2. 5-Minute High-Impact Demonstration Flow

### Act 1: Live Ingestion & Early Warning (PREDICT)
1. Navigate to the **"Pre-Incident Safety Center"** tab.
2. Note the live ingestion throughput counter (~30,000 eps at 100 streams, scaling to 1,000 streams with P95 latency < 75 ms).
3. In the **Stream Multiplexer**, click **"INJECT T-04 EXCURSION"**.
4. Observe the **Multi-Signal Early Warning Roster**:
   - T-04 Ammonia Cryogenic Tank immediately jumps from `NORMAL` to `PREVENTIVE`/`CRITICAL` (Score: 78.4 / 100).
   - Point out the **Multi-Signal Evidence Breakdown**: Mechanical Bearing Vibration (+25 pts), Pressure Excursion (+18 pts), Acoustic Micro-Leak (+12 pts), and Unsupervised Machine Learning (*Isolation Forest*) Anomaly probability (+25%).

### Act 2: Preventive Intervention & What-If Simulation (PREVENT)
1. Click **"PREVENTIVE WHAT-IF →"** on the T-04 card.
2. Review the physics-grounded engineering options generated by the **Preventive Engine**:
   - *ACT-T04-ISO: Remote-Operated Shut-Off Valves (ROSOV-04A/B) Actuation* (-32 pts $\Delta R$)
   - *ACT-T04-CURTAIN: High-Density Water Fog Knockdown Deluge Curtain* (-24 pts $\Delta R$)
3. Select both interventions and click **"SIMULATE INTERVENTIONS"**.
4. Point out the verified **PREDICT → INTERVENE → VERIFY** outcome:
   - Risk drops from **82 (CRITICAL)** to **34 (WATCH)** (-58.5% reduction).
   - Projected threat area drops by **85%**.
   - **6 workers protected** and **3 adjacent domino assets saved**.
5. Click **"REQUEST AUTHORIZATION"** to showcase the **Safety-Control Boundary**:
   - Highlight that AI never writes directly to safety valves.
   - Complete the 3-point checklist and sign off to generate the cryptographic token `AUTH-SIS-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`.

### Act 3: Consequence Screening & Risk Graph (PROTECT)
1. Switch to the **"Plant & Environmental Risk Graph"** sub-tab.
2. Point out that the system models process connections (PL-101 pipeline $\rightarrow$ PU-02 Ammonia Loop) and external consequence receptors:
   - *Gulf of Khambhat Marine Sanctuary*
   - *GIDC Stormwater & Industrial Effluent Drainage*
   - *Dahej Coastal Fishermen Settlement*
   - *State Highway 6 Logistics Corridor*

### Act 4: Emergency Response & Dynamic Evacuation (RESPOND)
1. Switch to the **"Command Map & Triage"** tab.
2. Point out the 2D GIS digital twin with the Gaussian dispersion plume (Red ERPG-3/IDLH, Orange ERPG-2, Yellow ERPG-1 threat zones) sliced across time ($T+0\text{s}, 30\text{s}, 60\text{s}, 120\text{s}$).
3. Switch to **"Safe Evacuation"**:
   - Show how the Dijkstra graph routing dynamically severs compromised internal roads and routes workers safely upwind to **Assembly Point 3** via **Gate 2**.
4. Switch to **"Tactical Response"**:
   - Show chemical-adaptive resource allocation: High-volume water bowser for ammonia vapor knockdown, Level A gas-tight suits, and triage ambulance staging.

### Act 5: Governance, Audit & Executive Briefing (LEARN)
1. Switch to **"Fire Pre-Plan PDF"** and click **"GENERATE FIRE PRE-PLAN PDF"** to produce an official vector-mapped ERDMP-compliant industrial safety document.
2. Open the **"Executive Situation Brief"** modal to display the 12-point executive summary.
3. Switch between roles in the top header (**Field Responder**, **HSE Commander**, **Plant Manager**, **Executive Authority**) to demonstrate role-based information abstraction with 100% synchronized canonical state.
